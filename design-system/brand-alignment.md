# Shared Movement Markets brand

`brand-alignment.json` records the common palette and canonical wordmark used by the landing page, client portal and IB portal. Dark surfaces are neutral black and charcoal, sourced from the landing-page design. The portals retain their existing light-mode surfaces and semantic success/error states.

The wordmark uses locally hosted Montserrat: Movement at 700, Markets at 300, one horizontal line, 15.5px type and a 24px proportional mark. Portal subtitles use 10px sentence case. Larger campaign and intro lockups may scale proportionally. The font is a 3.4 KB official subset containing only the wordmark letters; the SIL Open Font License is included under public/fonts. IB creative exports embed the same subset.

Account categories use distinct lime, blue, violet and amber markers and always retain their text labels. Dark and light variants preserve graphical contrast. Both the commission chart and its legend use these same colors.

The website hero uses an original 1254px square PNG of the canonical silver-and-lime two-panel gateway, displayed at its native aspect ratio in a responsive art column. The former hero image is retained in the repository. Navigation, trading data, authentication and referral calculations are unchanged.
